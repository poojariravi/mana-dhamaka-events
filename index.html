❤️ Mana Community, Mana Sambaram

Our traditions. Our friendships. Our celebrations.

Mana Dhamaka Events is a community initiative bringing Telugu families and friends together across Sweden. Our aim is to celebrate our culture, create new friendships and make beautiful memories through festivals, cultural activities, parties, summer picnics and family gatherings. Everyone is welcome to join the fun!
